  ②排程跳过（`ReviewPlanner.kt:456-467`）③自适应出题（`AdaptiveQuestionSelector.kt:143-164`：
  跳过 + 状态不新鲜时**拒出题**）④风险/弱点权重轴（`kcMasteryDropPressure`、`MasterySmoothing`、
